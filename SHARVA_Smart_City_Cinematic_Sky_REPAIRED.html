The problem

Services are scattered across offices, websites and phone lines. Citizens lose time, cannot reach help fast, and never know if a reported issue was fixed.

The solution

Sharva Smart City unifies transport, payments, water and power, waste, citizen services and emergency help. Find, pay, use, report and track from one screen on any device.
